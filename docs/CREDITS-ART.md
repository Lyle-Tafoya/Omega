# Art credits

The tiles in `assets/` come from **DawnLike**, made by **DragonDePlatino** using the
**DawnBringer** 16-colour palette. They're licensed under
[CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The original release is on OpenGameArt: <https://opengameart.org/content/dawnlike-16x16-universal-rogue-like-tileset-v181>

The sprite names and the split into separate files come from tommyettinger's
DawnLikeAtlas (<https://github.com/tommyettinger/DawnLikeAtlas>), also CC-BY 4.0.

The tiles were packed into a new atlas for Omega and are otherwise unchanged,
except that the Chaos Sea water is recoloured purple.

The interface font is DejaVu Sans Mono (Bitstream Vera licence), rendered into a bitmap.
